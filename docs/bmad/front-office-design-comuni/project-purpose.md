# Purpose

FixCity is a civic platform that lets residents report local problems, discover
public information, follow progress and participate in a transparent municipal
workflow. The front office is not a marketing landing page: it is a public service
where clarity, privacy, accessibility and recoverability matter more than novelty.

# Product promise

“Tell the municipality what is wrong, understand what happens next, and see progress
without losing control of your information.”

# Design consequence

DesignComuni is the external trust language and information architecture. FixCity's
brand and civic data enrich it, but do not replace its service structure. Every
visual decision must help a resident complete a task or understand public status.
